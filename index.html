Mobility

Two sessions, head to toe. Alternate A and B.

Two sessions · 100 balls · 60 minutes each · 2 alignment sticks
The 100-Ball Hour

Alternate them. A · Flip + posture fixes the release with irons and ends on iron targets. B · Wrists, turn + driver fixes the backswing and ends on scored drives. Both sessions are built from your 11 drills.

    Your routine · every target shot
    1. From behind the ball, pick a target and a spot on the ground a foot in front of the ball on that line.
    2. Make one rehearsal swing with the feel from the drills.
    3. Square the clubface to the spot first, then set your feet.
    4. One look at the target, one look at the ball, go. Under 8 seconds over the ball.

    Wrap up

    112 mph
    Driver club speed
    280–309 yd
    Driver carry, straight
    170–180 yd
    7-iron carry
    130–140 yd
    Pitching wedge carry

    How the faults connect

    Everything you've described fits one chain. Fix the start of it and the rest gets easier.

    1. 1

      Not enough body turnYour chest stops turning early, so your arms and wrists make up the length of the backswing.

    2. 2

      Wrist collapse at the topYour lead wrist over-hinges and cups, and your trail hand loosens, so the club drops past parallel.

    3. 3

      Re-grip in transitionYour hands tighten again on the way down, and that changes where the clubface points every swing.

    4. 4

      The hands have to rescue itTo square the face, your lead wrist cups and the clubhead passes your hands. That's the flip.

    5. 5

      Hook, or block when the timing is offSwing harder and the swing gets longer and the flip gets bigger. That's why the hook shows up with speed.

    Session B works on links 1 to 3: pre-set and turn, the forearm-stick check and the pause at the top. Session A works on links 4 and 5: toe up, flat trail hand and hold-off, with the stick behind your backside keeping room for your arms. Alternating them works on the whole chain twice a week.

    What I saw on the video

    One wedge swing, filmed from behind. The yellow lines show where your hips and cap were at address. Look at where they are at impact.

    Film these next

    Film a driver swing face on, phone square to your chest at hip height. That shows how long your backswing is and whether your lead wrist cups at impact. Film one at your normal swing and one after the pause drill, and send me both.

      Sign in with your Microsoft account to sync your data across all devices via OneDrive. Do this once per device. This same sign-in also grants access to your Outlook calendar for the Calendar page.

      Supabase sync
      Active

      An alternative to OneDrive for anyone who'd rather not use a Microsoft account — sign in with just an email and password. Handy if a friend wants to use this dashboard for themselves. Use one sync method at a time, not both, to avoid conflicting saves.

      How it works

      Click Sign in & pull to load your latest data from OneDrive, or Sign in & push to save your current data to OneDrive. Each button redirects you to Microsoft to sign in fresh — no password is remembered. Works identically on every device and browser. Signing in with Supabase (above) works the same way but with a normal email + password account instead, and becomes your active sync method until you sign in with the other one.
